THE HOLY OFFICE · PRE-PRODUCTION

Four visual systems

The same corridor, mounted inscription, and inquisitor are used in every comp so the difference is the system—not the composition. These are visual targets, not changes to the live renderer.

DIRECTION 01

Faceted shader pass

Lowest-cost retrofit: quantized diffuse light, reduced saturation, hard facets, and restrained amber fog over current geometry.

 DIRECTION 02

Hand-painted replacement

New low-poly NPC meshes and purpose-painted diffuse atlases. Highest fidelity to the intended reference, highest authoring cost.

 DIRECTION 03

Hybrid hero cast

Replace only conversation-distance NPCs; standardize shaders, lighting, and texture treatment everywhere else.

 DIRECTION 04

Severe retro budget

Make constraint the style: very low polygon counts, nearest-filtered textures, short fog, and low internal resolution.

Prototype files do not import game code and are safe to open directly from disk.
